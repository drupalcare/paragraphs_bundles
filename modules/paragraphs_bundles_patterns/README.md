# Paragraphs Bundles Patterns

Adds the PB Patterns picker under the Title on the content edit form. Pick a
card, press Insert pattern, and a finished section is added to the page with
sample words and pictures. Change them, then save.

Patterns are never required: every paragraph type works as it always did, and a
page you do not insert a pattern into is not affected.

Each pattern is designed and styled to work in a specific way. Changing its
configuration or code may affect its design or layout. Colors and spacing can be
adjusted with the bundle's own settings.

## Dependencies

- `paragraphs_bundles`
- `paragraphs`
- Core `file` and `media`

## How it works

- The picker shows on content types that have a Paragraphs field accepting at
  least one enabled bundle, to roles with the Insert PB Patterns permission.
- Every pattern has a card. When a bundle is not enabled, its cards stay
  visible but cannot be picked, and each names the module to turn on.
- Insert writes the section with the pattern's settings and adds its class to
  the CSS Classes field under Advanced. Everything can be changed afterwards.
- After Insert, a message at the top of the new section says what the pattern
  still needs from you. Video patterns need your video.
- Nothing is refused on save. If you change a section so that it no longer
  fits its pattern, you see the result and decide.

## Sample pictures and thumbnails

All of them are generated by this module's scripts, so they carry no outside
license. `scripts/make-sample-pictures.py` writes the designs and
`scripts/render-pictures.py` turns them into pictures and photographs each
pattern for its card. Sample media is saved once per site and reused.

## Theme colors

On Solo, APEX and their sub-themes, patterns take their colors from the region
they sit in. Every other theme keeps the patterns' neutral colors. Colors set on
the paragraph itself always win.

## Rules check

`python3 tools/check-pattern-rules.py` from this module's folder checks the
pattern files for the rules they must follow. `--self-test` proves each rule
can fail.

## Uninstall

Sections you already inserted keep their content and settings and return to
their bundle's normal look. The sample media stay in the media library.
